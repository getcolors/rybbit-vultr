Rybbit on Vultr

A single-node privacy-friendly analytics deployment at rybbit.bigconfig.website.

Architecture

Cloudflare and Caddy expose HTTPS. PostgreSQL 17, ClickHouse 24.8, Redis, and Rybbit internal application services remain private on one Amsterdam Vultr instance.

Recovery

Persistent disk survives restarts and daily R2 archives support disaster recovery. This single-node design is durable but not highly available.
